How it’s made

Each stroke is a real player’s recorded joint rotations from optical motion capture, replayed on a 1.8 m figure. The grip, the moment of contact and the incoming ball are solved together so every shot obeys published impact, drag, spin and bounce physics: the ball comes from a legal shot on the far side, bounces in, clears the net and lands in. The far-side player is the same captured forehand, turned around and timed to the ball.

Motion data: Tennis-MoCap, J.D. Pulgarín-Giraldo et al., licensed CC BY-SA 3.0. Pulgarín-Giraldo J.D. et al., A Similarity Indicator for Differentiating Kinematic Performance Between Qualified Tennis Players, LNCS 10125, pp. 309–317, 2017. Retargeted to this figure and combined with simulated ball flight.
